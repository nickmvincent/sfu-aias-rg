Paper Previews: Imaginations and Values in AI Research

Two paper previews about the goals and values shaping AI research. The presenters invited critical feedback on the first paper, which was under review at the time of the meeting.

Readings

AI for Just Work: Constructing Diverse Imaginations of AI beyond “Replacing Humans”

Weina Jin, Nicholas Vincent, and Ghassan Hamarneh

Calls on the AI technical community to diversify its collective imaginations of AI beyond replacing humans. The paper identifies limits in the mainstream imagination of AI and demonstrates an initial method for constructing alternatives in two AI tasks.

The Values Encoded in Machine Learning Research

Abeba Birhane, Pratyusha Kalluri, Dallas Card, William Agnew, Ravit Dotan, and Michelle Bao

Audits highly cited NeurIPS and ICML papers to identify the mainstream values guiding AI development and examines how those values can support the concentration of power.

Related reading from the discussion

Machine Learning that Matters

Kiri L. Wagstaff

Wagstaff’s 2012 ICML keynote paper argues that machine-learning research has lost touch with consequential problems in science and society. It highlights limitations in commonly used datasets and evaluation metrics, weak communication with originating domains, and the need to conduct research for greater real-world impact.
